Master's student in Computer Science University of Electronic Science and Technology of China

I am a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC), advised by Prof. Ping Hu and Prof. Xiaofeng Zhu. Previously, I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science from Guilin University of Electronic Technology (GUET).

My research focuses on image generation with diffusion and flow models, and physics-informed generative modeling for high-fidelity 3D perception. I am particularly interested in LiDAR sensing and measurement formation, as well as realistic generation and restoration under adverse weather. Additionally, I am broadly interested in and open to exploring world models and robotics.

I am actively applying to Computer Science PhD programs for Fall 2027. My TOEFL iBT score is 100/120 (5.0/6.0; Reading 5.0, Listening 5.0, Speaking 4.5, Writing 5.5; CEFR C1). If you are recruiting PhD students and see a potential fit with my research interests, please feel free to contact me.

Publications

Structure-to-Intensity Diffusion for Adverse-Weather LiDAR Generation

Peiyang Ni, Longyu Yang, Lu Zhang, Kuniaki Saito, Yap-Peng Tan, Fumin Shen, Heng Tao Shen, Xiaofeng Zhu, and Ping Hu

CVPR 2026

A physics-grounded diffusion framework that explicitly factorizes LiDAR generation by reconstructing 3D geometric structure first and generating reflectance intensity conditioned on recovered geometry, supported by real-prior adverse-weather simulation.

Paper Project Page Code

A2CFlow: Range-Aware Rectified Flow for Efficient Adverse-Weather LiDAR Restoration

Peiyang Ni, Xiaorui Sun, Lu Zhang, Heng Tao Shen, Xiaofeng Zhu, and Ping Hu

Revised and Resubmitted to IEEE RA-L

A conditional rectified-flow framework that restores clean LiDAR scans from a single adverse-weather observation, scheduling transport progress by range-dependent sensing reliability to jointly suppress clutter and reconstruct absent geometry.
