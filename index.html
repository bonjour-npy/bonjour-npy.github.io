    Biography

    I am Peiyang Ni (倪培洋), a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC), advised by Prof. Ping Hu and Prof. Xiaofeng Zhu. I received my B.Eng. degree in Computer Science from Guilin University of Electronic Technology (GUET).

    My research focuses on image generation with diffusion and flow models, and physics-informed generative modeling for high-fidelity 3D perception. I am particularly interested in LiDAR sensing and measurement formation, as well as realistic generation and restoration under adverse weather.
